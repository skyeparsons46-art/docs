Publishing your story on Story puts your work in front of readers around the world. Before you hit publish, take a few minutes to configure your story's visibility, content rating, genre tags, and cover image — these settings determine how readers discover your story and what they see when they find it.

## Visibility Settings

Your story's visibility controls who can access it. Choose the level that fits your intentions:

- **Public** — Your story appears in search results and recommendations. Anyone on Story can find it and read it without needing a link.
- **Unlisted** — Your story does not appear in search or recommendations. Only people you share the direct link with can read it. Useful for sharing with beta readers or a private audience.
- **Private** — Only you can see your story. Use this while you're still developing an idea or before you're ready to share it with anyone.

You can change your visibility setting at any time from the story settings panel without affecting your published chapters.

## Content Ratings

Select the rating that accurately reflects the content of your story:

- **General** — Suitable for all ages. No violence, mature themes, or explicit content.
- **Teen** — Suitable for readers 13 and up. May include mild violence, light romantic themes, or moderate language.
- **Mature (17+)** — Contains adult content, graphic violence, or explicit themes. Readers must confirm their age before accessing Mature-rated stories.

<Warning>
  Stories default to the **General** rating. If your story contains mature themes, you must explicitly select the **Mature (17+)** rating before publishing. Publishing Mature content under an incorrect rating violates Story's content guidelines and may result in your story being removed.
</Warning>

## Genre Tags

Genre tags connect your story to readers who are actively looking for the kind of content you write. You can add **up to 5 genre tags** to each story. Tags are used by Story's search engine and recommendation algorithm to surface your work to the right audience.

Choose tags that genuinely reflect your story's genre and themes — for example, *Fantasy*, *Romance*, *Mystery*, *Science Fiction*, or *Horror*. Avoid adding tags that don't match your content, as this leads to poor reader experiences and can affect your story's visibility in recommendations.

## Cover Image

Your cover is the first thing a reader sees. A compelling cover image significantly increases click-through rates and follows your story across the platform — in search results, reader libraries, and your author profile.

- **Recommended dimensions:** 1600 × 2400 px
- **Aspect ratio:** 2:3 (portrait)
- **Maximum file size:** 10 MB
- **Supported formats:** JPG, PNG, WebP

Upload your cover from the story settings panel by clicking **"Upload Cover"** and selecting your image file. Story crops and optimizes the image automatically for all display sizes.

## Scheduled Publishing

If you want your story to go live at a specific time — for a launch event, a time zone–friendly release, or a coordinated social announcement — use scheduled publishing. Set a **future date and time** in the story settings panel, and Story will automatically publish your story at that moment without any further action from you.

To schedule a release, open story settings, toggle **"Schedule publish"**, and pick your date and time. The story remains in draft until the scheduled time arrives.

## Publishing Your Story

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open story settings">
    From your story dashboard, click **"Story Settings"** in the top-right menu to open the settings panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your visibility, content rating, and genre tags">
    Choose your **Visibility** level, select the appropriate **Content Rating**, and add up to 5 **Genre Tags** that describe your story.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your cover image">
    Click **"Upload Cover"** and select a portrait-orientation image (1600 × 2400 px recommended, max 10 MB).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose to publish now or schedule">
    To publish immediately, click **"Publish Story"**. To schedule, toggle **"Schedule publish"**, set your date and time, then click **"Schedule"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your story">
    Once published, copy your story's public URL from the settings panel and share it with your audience on social media, in your newsletter, or anywhere your readers will see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
