Chapters are the building blocks of your story on Story. Breaking your narrative into chapters makes it easier for readers to track their progress, return to where they left off, and look forward to new releases. You manage all of your chapters — drafts, published, and upcoming — from your story dashboard.

## Adding a Chapter

To add a new chapter, navigate to your **story dashboard** and click **"Add Chapter"**. A new chapter entry appears in your chapter list, prompting you to enter a title. Give it a working title — you can rename it at any time from the chapter settings panel.

Once you've named your chapter, click it to open it in the editor and start writing.

## Reordering Chapters

Your chapter list in the sidebar reflects the order readers will experience your story. To change that order, **drag and drop** any chapter to a new position in the list. Grab the drag handle (the six-dot icon) on the left side of any chapter row and move it up or down.

Your changes are saved automatically, and readers immediately see the updated order.

## Chapter Drafts vs Published

Every chapter carries its own **draft/published state**, independent of the overall story. This gives you precise control over what your readers can access:

- **Draft** — The chapter is saved and visible to you in your dashboard, but hidden from readers.
- **Published** — The chapter is live and readable by anyone with access to your story (based on your story's visibility setting).

You can publish chapters one at a time as you finish them, or write several in draft and publish them all at once using the **"Publish All Drafts"** button in the story dashboard.

## Chapter Settings

Each chapter has its own settings panel, accessible by clicking the **gear icon** next to the chapter title. From here you can configure:

- **Chapter number** — Customize the display number (e.g. "Prologue", "Chapter 1", "Interlude").
- **Author note** — Write a personal note to your readers. Choose whether it appears **before** or **after** the chapter text. Author notes are a great place to share your inspiration, thank readers, or tease the next chapter.
- **Word count goal** — Set a target word count for the chapter. Your progress toward the goal is displayed in the editor while you write.

## Publishing Your First Chapter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write your chapter content in the editor">
    Open the chapter from your story dashboard and write your content. The editor auto-saves your draft every 30 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review with the Preview button">
    Click the **Preview** button in the top-right corner of the editor to see exactly how your chapter will appear to readers, including formatting, images, and author notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Publish Chapter in the chapter settings panel">
    Open the chapter settings panel and click **"Publish Chapter"**. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears. The chapter status changes from Draft to Published immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Readers are notified if they're following your story">
    Anyone who has followed your story receives a notification that a new chapter is available. They can jump straight to it from their notification feed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  There is no limit on the number of chapters you can add to a story. Write as many chapters as your narrative needs — Story scales with you from a short story to an epic series.
</Info>
