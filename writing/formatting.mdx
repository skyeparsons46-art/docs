Good formatting guides your readers through your story without drawing attention to itself. Story's editor supports a rich set of formatting tools — from bold and italic emphasis to embedded images and scene-dividing horizontal rules — all accessible from the toolbar or via familiar keyboard shortcuts.

## Text Styles

Apply inline styles to words and phrases to add emphasis or convey meaning:

| Style | Toolbar Button | Markdown Shortcut |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Bold** | **B** | `**text**` |
| *Italic* | *I* | `*text*` |
| <u>Underline</u> | U | — |
| ~~Strikethrough~~ | S̶ | `~~text~~` |

Type the markdown shortcut directly in the editor and Story will convert it automatically on the fly. For underline and strikethrough, use the toolbar buttons.

## Headings

Headings give your story structure and help readers navigate longer works:

- **H1** — Reserved for your story's title, which Story sets automatically. Avoid using H1 inside chapter body text.
- **H2** — Use for major section breaks within a chapter, such as a shift in time or location.
- **H3** — Use for sub-sections within an H2 block when you need a third level of hierarchy.

Select the text you want to style, then choose the heading level from the toolbar dropdown or type the corresponding Markdown prefix (`##` for H2, `###` for H3) at the start of a new line.

## Lists

Lists work well for in-story inventories, rules, letters, or any content that benefits from visual separation:

- **Unordered lists** — Click the bullet-list button in the toolbar, or start a line with `- ` or `* `.
- **Ordered lists** — Click the numbered-list button, or start a line with `1. `.

You can nest lists up to **2 levels deep** by pressing **Tab** to indent an item and **Shift+Tab** to outdent it.

## Blockquotes

Blockquotes are ideal for epigraphs at the start of a chapter, quoted letters or documents within a story, and stylized dialogue passages.

To create a blockquote, click the **blockquote button** in the toolbar, or type `> ` at the beginning of a new line:

```
> "It was a bright cold day in April, and the clocks were striking thirteen."
```

The editor renders the blockquote with a left border and indented styling, visually separating it from the surrounding prose.

## Images

You can embed images directly into the body of your chapter:

- **Drag and drop** an image file from your computer onto the editor canvas.
- **Click the Insert Image button** in the toolbar and select a file from your device.

**Supported formats:** JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP  
**Maximum file size:** 5 MB per image

After uploading, you can add optional alt text to make your story more accessible.

<Note>
  All images you upload are automatically hosted on Story's CDN. You don't need to manage your own image hosting — Story handles delivery, optimization, and availability for your readers worldwide.
</Note>

## Dividers

Use a horizontal rule to separate scenes, signal a passage of time, or mark a point-of-view shift without introducing a new heading.

Type `---` on its own line and press **Enter**, or click the **divider button** in the toolbar. The editor renders it as a full-width horizontal line.
