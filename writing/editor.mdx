The Story editor is your core writing tool — a clean, powerful environment built to keep you in the flow of your narrative. Whether you're drafting your first chapter or polishing your final scene, the editor gives you everything you need without getting in your way.

## The Writing Interface

When you open a chapter, you land directly in the editor canvas. Along the top sits the **toolbar**, which contains all the formatting controls you need:

- **Headings** — Apply H1, H2, or H3 heading styles to structure your story
- **Bold / Italic** — Emphasize words and phrases with inline styles
- **Lists** — Create unordered (bullet) or ordered (numbered) lists
- **Blockquotes** — Offset dialogue, epigraphs, or excerpts with a styled indent
- **Images** — Insert images inline with your text via the image button

To the right of the toolbar you'll find the **word count display**, which updates in real time as you type. Next to it is the **distraction-free mode toggle** — a button that hides the toolbar and all surrounding chrome so only your text remains visible.

## Auto-Save

Story saves your draft automatically every **30 seconds**, so you never lose your work. A subtle "Saved" indicator appears in the top bar each time an auto-save completes.

If you want to save immediately, press **Ctrl+S** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd+S** (Mac) at any time. Manual saves are instant and bypass the 30-second timer entirely.

<Tip>
  Get into the habit of hitting Ctrl/Cmd+S after finishing a paragraph. Even though auto-save has you covered, a manual save gives you immediate peace of mind.
</Tip>

## Focus Mode

Focus mode removes every piece of UI chrome — the toolbar, sidebar, navigation bar, and word count display — leaving only your text on a clean background. It's ideal for long writing sessions where interruptions break your concentration.

To enter focus mode, press **F11** or click the **fullscreen button** in the top-right corner of the editor. Press **F11** again or **Escape** to exit.

<Tip>
  Use focus mode whenever you sit down for a dedicated writing session. Eliminating visual noise makes it significantly easier to stay in your story's world and maintain your creative momentum.
</Tip>

## Keyboard Shortcuts

Speed up your workflow with these keyboard shortcuts:

| Action | Windows / Linux | Mac |
|--------|----------------|-----|
| Bold | Ctrl+B | Cmd+B |
| Italic | Ctrl+I | Cmd+I |
| Insert Link | Ctrl+K | Cmd+K |
| Undo | Ctrl+Z | Cmd+Z |
| Save | Ctrl+S | Cmd+S |
