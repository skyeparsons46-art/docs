Your Story profile is the public face of your writing career on the platform. A complete, well-crafted profile helps readers learn who you are, builds trust in your work, and makes it easier for new followers to discover your stories. Follow the steps below to finish setting up your account after you sign in for the first time.

## Complete Your Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a Profile Photo">
    Navigate to **Settings → Profile** and click the profile photo placeholder. Select an image from your device to upload. For the best results, use a square image that is at least **400×400 pixels** — Story will automatically crop and resize it to fit the circular avatar shown on your author page and next to your comments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Your Bio">
    In the **Bio** field, write a short description of yourself as a writer. Your bio has a **150 character limit**, so keep it punchy and focused — mention your favorite genres, your writing style, or what inspires you. Your bio appears prominently on your public author profile and in search results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a Username">
    Choose a unique username that will form your public URL handle. For example, if your username is `alexwrites`, your author page will be accessible at `story.app/@alexwrites`. Usernames may contain letters, numbers, and underscores. Pick something memorable that readers will associate with your work.

    <Note>
      You can only change your username **once every 30 days**. Choose carefully — all existing links to your profile will update automatically, but readers who bookmarked your old URL will need to find you again.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Genres">
    Select up to **5 genres** from the genre list in your profile settings. Story uses your chosen genres to recommend your work to readers who follow those categories. Pick the genres that best represent the stories you write or plan to write — you can update these at any time from your profile settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Account Security

Keeping your account secure protects both your work and your readers. Story offers two layers of protection you should set up right away.

**Change Your Password**

Go to **Settings → Security → Change Password**. Enter your current password, then type and confirm your new password. Story requires passwords to be at least 8 characters and include a mix of letters and numbers. Click **Update Password** to save.

**Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

For an extra layer of security, enable 2FA under **Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication**. Story supports authenticator apps such as Google Authenticator and Authy. Scan the QR code displayed on screen with your authenticator app, then enter the six-digit code it generates to confirm the setup. Once enabled, you'll be prompted for a code each time you sign in from a new device.

<Note>
  Store your 2FA backup codes in a safe place when you set up two-factor authentication. If you lose access to your authenticator app, you'll need these codes to recover your account.
</Note>

For advanced profile customization — including social links, featured stories, and notification preferences — visit [Settings → Profile](/settings/profile).
