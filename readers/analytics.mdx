Knowing how readers discover and experience your stories helps you write more of what resonates. Story's analytics dashboard puts that information at your fingertips — tracking everything from how many times your story has been opened to how far through each chapter the average reader gets. Use these insights to refine your writing, time your releases, and grow your audience with confidence.

<Info>
  Analytics data updates every 24 hours. Numbers you see today reflect activity up to the previous day, so check back regularly to track trends over time.
</Info>

## Accessing Analytics

To open the analytics dashboard for any story:

1. Go to your **Dashboard**.
2. Select **My Stories** from the left sidebar.
3. Find the story you want to analyze and click the **Analytics icon** (bar chart symbol) next to its title.

The analytics dashboard opens with an overview of all key metrics for that story.

## Key Metrics

The analytics dashboard surfaces five core metrics to help you understand how your story is performing:

- **Total Reads** — The total number of times your story has been opened across all chapters and all time periods. This is your broadest measure of reach.
- **Unique Visitors** — The count of distinct readers who have viewed your story, regardless of how many times each person visited. Compare this with Total Reads to gauge how often the same readers return.
- **Chapter Completion Rate** — The percentage of readers who finish reading each individual chapter. A high completion rate signals strong chapter-level engagement; a sudden drop points to where readers lose interest.
- **Follower Growth** — A time-series chart showing how your follower count has changed over the selected date range, including spikes after new releases or social shares.
- **Average Read Time** — The estimated average amount of time readers spend actively reading per session, calculated from engagement signals. Use this to understand whether your chapter length feels right for your audience.

<Tip>
  Pay close attention to your **Chapter Completion Rate** across chapters. A significant drop on a specific chapter often signals a pacing issue, a confusing plot point, or a chapter that's running too long — all actionable feedback you can use to revise and improve retention.
</Tip>

## Date Ranges

Filter all analytics metrics by a specific time window using the **Date Range** selector at the top of the dashboard. The available options are:

- **Last 7 days** — ideal for tracking the immediate impact of a new chapter release.
- **Last 30 days** — a good default for monitoring monthly momentum.
- **Last 90 days** — useful for spotting longer-term trends and seasonal patterns.
- **All Time** — see your story's complete performance history since it was first published.

Switching date ranges updates every metric and chart on the page instantly.

## Traffic Sources

The **Traffic Sources** section of the dashboard breaks down where your readers are coming from, so you can focus your sharing efforts on the channels that drive the most traffic. Story tracks four source categories:

- **Direct Link** — readers who navigated to your story using your `story.app/@username/story-slug` URL directly, such as from an email or a bookmarked link.
- **Search** — readers who found your story through a search engine or Story's internal search feature.
- **Social Media** — readers arriving from shared posts on X (Twitter), Facebook, Bluesky, or other social platforms.
- **Recommendations** — readers directed to your story by Story's algorithmic recommendations, "Readers also enjoyed" suggestions, or curated lists.

Use this breakdown to identify your strongest discovery channel and double down on it — for example, if social media drives the most traffic, prioritize sharing new chapters across your social profiles on release day.
