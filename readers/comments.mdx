Comments and reactions turn your story into a conversation. When readers can leave feedback, share their reactions, and reply to one another, they invest more deeply in your work and keep coming back. Story gives you full control over how comments work on your stories — from enabling them on a per-chapter basis to moderating what appears publicly.

<Note>
  Readers must be signed in to a Story account to leave comments or reactions. This helps keep your community accountable and reduces spam.
</Note>

## Enabling Comments

Turn comments on or off at any time from your story's settings. Go to **Story Settings → Comments** and toggle the **Enable Comments** switch. You can apply this setting at two levels:

- **Per story** — enable or disable comments across the entire story at once.
- **Per chapter** — open a specific chapter's settings and toggle comments independently, useful if you want discussion on some chapters but not others.

Changes take effect immediately. Disabling comments hides existing comments from readers but does not delete them; re-enabling restores them.

## Moderating Comments

Keep your story's comment section welcoming by reviewing what readers post. In **Story Settings → Comments → Moderation**, you can turn on the optional moderation queue, which holds new comments for your approval before they appear publicly.

From the moderation queue you can:

- **Approve** a comment to make it visible to all readers.
- **Reject** a comment to discard it without notifying the reader.
- **Delete** any existing comment directly from the comment thread on your story page.
- **Ban a reader** from commenting on your story by selecting **Ban User** from the options menu next to their comment.

<Warning>
  If a comment violates Story's community guidelines, use the **flag icon** on the comment to report it to Story's Trust & Safety team. Reporting is available to all readers, not just authors, so your community can help keep conversations respectful.
</Warning>

## Reactions

Readers can respond to your story or individual chapters with one of six emoji reactions:

❤️ &nbsp; 😂 &nbsp; 😮 &nbsp; 😢 &nbsp; 😡 &nbsp; 👏

Reaction counts appear on both your story's main page and on each individual chapter page. They give you a quick, at-a-glance sense of how readers are responding emotionally to your work — and they give readers a lightweight way to engage even when they don't have time to write a full comment.

## Replying to Comments

Click **Reply** on any comment to respond directly and start a threaded discussion. Nested replies are supported up to **3 levels deep**, keeping conversations organized without becoming unwieldy.

Threads are visible to all readers, so thoughtful replies from you as the author can deepen engagement and show your audience that you value their feedback. Readers also receive a notification when you reply to their comment.

## Comment Notifications

Stay on top of reader feedback without checking your story page constantly. Story notifies you whenever a reader leaves a new comment on any of your stories or chapters.

Configure how you receive these alerts in **Settings → Notifications**:

- **Email notifications** — receive a summary email for new comments.
- **In-app notifications** — see a badge in the notification bell at the top of your Story dashboard.

You can adjust the frequency and scope of notifications at any time, including options to receive alerts only for top-level comments or for all replies in a thread.
