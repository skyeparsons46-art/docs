Once your story is published, the next step is getting it in front of readers. Story gives you several ways to share your work — from a permanent link you can drop anywhere, to built-in social media integrations and embeddable preview widgets. The more places your story appears, the more readers you attract.

## Story Link

Every published story automatically receives a permanent, shareable URL in the format:

```
story.app/@username/story-slug
```

This link works as soon as you publish. To copy it, open your story's dashboard, click the **Share** button at the top right, and select **Copy Link**. You can paste this URL anywhere — emails, newsletters, forums, or anywhere else your audience already gathers.

<Tip>
  Customize your story slug in **Story Settings → URL Slug** to create a clean, memorable link that's easier to share and better for search engine visibility. For example, `story.app/@janedoe/the-last-lighthouse` is far more descriptive than a default generated slug.
</Tip>

## Share to Social Media

Story includes built-in share buttons for **X (Twitter)**, **Facebook**, and **Bluesky**. Find them in the Share panel on your story dashboard or at the bottom of any published story page.

When you share, Story automatically generates a rich preview card that includes your story's cover image, title, and description — so your post stands out in social feeds right away. No extra setup is required; the preview card is built from the cover image and description you set in Story Settings.

## Embed Your Story

Place a story preview widget directly on your own website or blog using the Story embed code. The widget displays your cover image, title, and a short excerpt, giving visitors a compelling preview before they click through to read.

To get your embed code, go to your story dashboard, open the **Share** panel, and click **Copy Embed Code**. Then paste the snippet into your site's HTML:

```html
<iframe
  src="https://story.app/embed/@username/story-slug"
  width="300"
  height="450"
  frameborder="0">
</iframe>
```

You can adjust the `width` and `height` values to fit your page layout. The embedded widget links directly to your story on Story, so any reader who clicks it is taken straight to your published work.

## Follow & Notifications

Readers can follow your profile directly from any of your story pages. When someone follows you, they automatically receive email notifications whenever you release a new chapter or publish a new story — so your most engaged readers never miss an update.

Encourage readers to follow you by mentioning it in your author's note or at the end of each chapter. A growing follower count also means a built-in audience ready to read your next release the moment it goes live.
