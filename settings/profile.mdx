Your Story profile is how readers discover and connect with you. A complete, compelling profile helps you grow your audience — readers are more likely to follow authors who have a clear bio, a recognizable photo, and active social links. Keep your profile up to date as your work evolves.

## Editing Your Profile

To edit your profile, go to **Settings → Profile**. You can update the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Display Name** | The name shown publicly across Story — your readers' first impression of you. |
| **Username** | Your unique `@handle` used in your author page URL and @mentions. |
| **Bio** | A short description of you and your writing (150 characters maximum). |
| **Website URL** | A link to your personal site, newsletter, or any external page you want to share. |
| **Location** | An optional city, country, or region shown on your public profile. |

After making changes, click **Save Profile** to apply them.

<Note>
  You can change your username once every 30 days. Choose carefully — any existing links to your old `@handle` will no longer resolve after a username change.
</Note>

## Profile Photo

Your profile photo puts a face to your name and makes your author page and comments instantly recognizable. To upload a photo:

1. Go to **Settings → Profile**.
2. Click your current photo (or the placeholder avatar) to open the upload dialog.
3. Select a JPG or PNG image from your device.
4. Adjust the crop if needed, then click **Save Photo**.

For the best results, use an image that is at least **400 × 400 pixels**. Files must be **JPG or PNG** format and no larger than **2 MB**. Your photo appears on your public author page and next to every comment you post.

## Social Links

Connect your social accounts to let readers find you beyond Story. You can add links to the following platforms:

- **X (Twitter)**
- **Instagram**
- **TikTok**
- **Personal website**

To add or update your social links, go to **Settings → Profile → Social Links**, enter the full URL for each account you want to display, and click **Save**. These links appear prominently on your public author profile so readers can follow your work across platforms.

## Author Page

Every Story author has a public page at `story.app/@username`. Your author page displays:

- Your **profile photo** and **display name**
- Your **bio** and **location**
- All of your **published stories**
- Your **follower count** and the accounts you follow

Share your author page URL in your social bios, email signature, or anywhere you want new readers to find your work.
