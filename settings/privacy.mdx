Your privacy is yours to define. Story gives you granular control over who can see your profile and stories, how readers can interact with you, and what happens to your personal data. Review the settings in **Settings → Privacy** to make sure your account works exactly the way you want it to.

## Profile Visibility

Your profile visibility determines who can view your author page and the stories listed on it. Choose from three levels:

- **Public** — anyone on Story (or the open web) can view your profile and discover your work.
- **Followers Only** — only readers who follow you can see your full profile and story list.
- **Private** — your author page and stories are hidden from everyone who does not follow you. New readers must send a follow request before gaining access.

To change your profile visibility, go to **Settings → Privacy → Profile Visibility** and select your preferred option, then click **Save**.

## Story Visibility

Individual stories have their own visibility settings that work independently of your profile visibility. Each story can be set to:

- **Public** — visible to anyone.
- **Unlisted** — accessible only to readers with a direct link; not shown on your author page or in search results.
- **Private** — visible only to you.

You can change the visibility of a story at any time from your story's settings panel. For full details, see [Publishing Your Story](/writing/publishing).

## Blocking Readers

Blocking prevents a specific account from viewing your profile and stories, leaving comments, or following you. To block a reader:

1. Navigate to the reader's profile.
2. Click the **⋯** (more options) menu in the top-right corner of their profile.
3. Select **Block**.

The block takes effect immediately. Blocked readers are not notified that they have been blocked. To review or remove your blocks, go to **Settings → Privacy → Blocked Accounts**.

## Reading History

Story keeps a private reading history so you can easily pick up where you left off. This history is **visible only to you** and is never shared with other users or displayed on your public profile.

To clear your reading history, go to **Settings → Privacy → Clear History** and click **Clear All**. This removes all entries from your history log but does not affect the stories themselves or your follows.

## Data Export

You can download a complete copy of your account data at any time. The export includes all your stories, chapters, comments, profile information, and account settings packaged as a **ZIP file**.

To request an export:

1. Go to **Settings → Privacy → Export My Data**.
2. Click **Request Export**.
3. Story will send a download link to your email address once the file is ready.

Processing your export can take **up to 24 hours** depending on the size of your account. You will receive an email notification as soon as the download is available.

## Account Deletion

If you want to permanently remove your presence from Story, you can delete your account from **Settings → Privacy → Delete Account**. Deleting your account removes your profile, all published stories and chapters, comments, and all associated data from Story's systems.

<Warning>
  Account deletion is **permanent and irreversible**. Once you confirm deletion, your stories, chapters, comments, and all account data cannot be recovered. If you are unsure, consider setting your profile to **Private** or exporting your data first before proceeding.
</Warning>
