Story keeps you in the loop whenever something important happens — a new follower, a reader comment, or a fresh chapter from an author you love. You have full control over which notifications you receive, where they appear, and how often they arrive. Head to **Settings → Notifications** to customize your preferences at any time.

## Notification Types

Story sends notifications for the following activity types:

- **New Follower** — someone starts following you on Story. Use this to track your audience growth and discover new readers.
- **New Comment** — a reader leaves a comment on one of your stories or chapters. Stay on top of reader feedback without having to check each story manually.
- **Comment Reply** — someone replies directly to a comment you posted. Keeps conversations threaded and easy to follow.
- **Story Update** — a story you follow publishes a new chapter. Never miss an update from the authors you read.
- **Platform Announcements** — product news, new feature launches, and important updates from the Story team.

## Email vs. In-App

Each notification type can be toggled independently for two delivery channels:

- **In-App** — notifications appear in the bell (🔔) menu in the Story header while you browse the platform.
- **Email** — notifications are sent to the email address associated with your account.

To adjust these settings, go to **Settings → Notifications** and use the toggle switches to turn email and in-app delivery on or off for each notification type individually. For example, you might want in-app notifications for new comments but only email notifications for platform announcements.

## Digest Mode

Instead of receiving an email for every individual event, Digest Mode bundles all your activity into a single summary message. Choose between:

- **Daily Digest** — one email each day covering all activity from the previous 24 hours.
- **Weekly Digest** — one email each week covering all activity from the previous 7 days.

To enable Digest Mode, go to **Settings → Notifications → Email Preferences** and select your preferred digest frequency. Digest Mode applies to all notification types that have email delivery enabled.

<Tip>
  If your inbox is getting busy, switch to Weekly Digest Mode. You'll still catch every comment, follow, and chapter update — just in one tidy summary instead of a stream of individual emails.
</Tip>

## Unsubscribe

Every notification email from Story includes an **Unsubscribe** link in the footer. Clicking it takes you directly to your email preferences where you can turn off that specific notification type.

To disable all email notifications at once, go to **Settings → Notifications → Email Preferences** and toggle off **All Email Notifications**. This stops all Story emails immediately while keeping your in-app notifications active. You can re-enable email notifications at any time from the same page.
