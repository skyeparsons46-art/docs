**Story** is a modern writing platform designed for anyone who has a story to tell. Whether you're a seasoned novelist, a hobbyist fan-fiction writer, or someone putting words to page for the very first time, Story gives you a distraction-free editor, powerful organization tools, and a built-in audience to share your work with. You write; Story handles the rest — from formatting and chapter management to reader discovery and engagement analytics — so you can focus entirely on your craft.

## Key Features

Story is packed with everything a writer needs to create, grow, and connect.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Rich Text Editor" icon="pen-nib">
    Write with a beautiful, distraction-free editor that supports headings, bold, italic, block quotes, and more. Your formatting is preserved exactly as you intend it, every time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chapter Organization" icon="book">
    Structure your stories with ease. Create, reorder, and manage chapters in a clean sidebar so your narrative always flows the way you planned.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Publishing & Sharing" icon="rocket">
    Publish your work to a global audience with a single click. Share a unique story link across social media, email, or anywhere else your readers hang out.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reader Analytics" icon="chart-bar">
    Understand your audience with detailed read counts, chapter-level engagement data, and follower growth metrics — all in one easy-to-read dashboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Comments & Reactions" icon="users">
    Build a community around your writing. Readers can leave inline comments and reactions on individual chapters, giving you direct, meaningful feedback.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Privacy Controls" icon="lock">
    You decide who reads your work. Set any story or chapter to Public, Private, or Unlisted, and change visibility at any time without affecting your drafts.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
