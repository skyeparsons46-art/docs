## Get Started in Four Steps

New to Story? Follow these steps to go from zero to published in no time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Head to [story.app/signup](https://story.app/signup) and register with your email address. You'll receive a verification email — confirm it to activate your account and unlock all features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a Story">
    From your dashboard, click **New Story** and give your work a title, a short description, and a genre. This becomes the home for all the chapters you'll write.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Your First Chapter">
    Open the Story editor and start writing. Use headings, bold, italic, and other formatting tools to shape your prose exactly the way you envision it. Save your progress as a draft at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    When you're ready to share your work, click **Publish**. Choose your visibility — Public, Private, or Unlisted — and share your unique story link with readers worldwide.
  </Step>
</Steps>
