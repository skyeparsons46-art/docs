This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a blank page to a published story. By the end, you'll have a Story account, a new story with your first chapter, and a shareable link you can send to readers right away. No prior experience required — let's get started.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Go to [story.app/signup](https://story.app/signup) and enter your email address and a secure password. After you submit the form, Story will send a verification email to the address you provided. Open that email and click the confirmation link to activate your account. Once confirmed, you'll be taken directly to your writer dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a New Story">
    From your dashboard, click the **New Story** button in the top-right corner. Enter a title for your story and, optionally, a short description that tells readers what to expect. Select one or more genres and add tags to help new readers discover your work through search and recommendations. Click **Create** to save your new story and open its chapter list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Your First Chapter">
    Inside your story, click **Add Chapter** to open the Story editor. Give your chapter a title, then start writing in the main content area. Use the toolbar to format your text with headings, **bold**, *italic*, block quotes, and horizontal rules. When you're happy with what you've written — or just want to take a break — click **Save Draft** to preserve your progress without making it visible to readers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish Your Story">
    When you're ready to go live, click the **Publish** button at the top of the editor. Choose a visibility setting for your story:

    - **Public** — Anyone on Story can discover and read your work.
    - **Unlisted** — Only people with your direct link can read it.
    - **Private** — Only you can see it.

    Confirm your choice and Story will generate your unique story URL. Copy the link and share it with your readers anywhere — social media, your newsletter, or a direct message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Story automatically saves your work as a draft every 30 seconds while you're writing, so you never lose progress. Look for the **Saved** indicator in the top bar to confirm your latest changes are secure.
</Tip>

For more detail on configuring your author profile and account security after signing up, see the [Account Setup](/account-setup) guide.
