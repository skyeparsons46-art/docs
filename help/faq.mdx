Whether you're just getting started or you've been publishing on Story for a while, you probably have questions. Below you'll find answers to the most common ones — organized by topic so you can find what you need quickly.

## Getting Started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Story free to use?">
    Yes, Story offers a free tier that gives you access to the core writing and publishing tools. Premium features — including advanced analytics, scheduled publishing, and an ad-free reading experience — are available on the paid plan. You can upgrade at any time from **Settings → Billing**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need an account to read stories?">
    No. Any story that an author has published as **Public** is readable without an account. However, you do need an account to comment on stories, follow authors, or save stories to your reading list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What file types can I import?">
    You can import **.docx** and **.txt** files directly into a new story or an existing chapter. To do so, open the editor and select **Import** from the toolbar menu, then choose your file.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Writing & Publishing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many stories can I create?">
    Free accounts can have up to **5 published stories** at a time. If you need more, you can upgrade to a paid plan, which gives you unlimited published stories. Drafts do not count toward your published story limit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I schedule a chapter release?">
    Yes. Open the chapter you want to schedule and go to **Chapter Settings**. Set a future publish date and time, then save. Your chapter will auto-publish at that moment without any further action needed from you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are my drafts saved automatically?">
    Yes. The editor auto-saves your work every 30 seconds. You can also save manually at any time by pressing **Ctrl+S** on Windows or **Cmd+S** on Mac. The save status indicator in the top bar lets you know when your latest changes have been saved.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account & Privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my username?">
    Yes, you can change your username once every **30 days**. To update it, go to **Settings → Profile → Username**, enter your new username, and save your changes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    Go to **Settings → Privacy → Delete Account** and follow the confirmation steps. Please note that account deletion is **permanent and cannot be undone** — all your stories, drafts, comments, and account data will be removed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can see my private stories?">
    **Private** stories are visible only to you while you're logged in. **Unlisted** stories are not surfaced in search or recommendations, but anyone who has the direct link can view them. **Public** stories are visible to all readers.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    Story accepts the following payment methods:

    - **Credit cards** — Visa, Mastercard, and American Express
    - **PayPal**

    All transactions are processed securely. Story does not store your full card details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my subscription?">
    Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from **Settings → Billing**. After you cancel, your paid plan remains active until the end of your current billing period. You won't be charged again after that.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
