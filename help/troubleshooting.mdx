Running into a problem on Story? Most issues can be resolved in just a few steps. Work through the relevant section below to get back on track — and if nothing here solves your issue, reach out to the support team at the bottom of this page.

## Editor Issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My changes aren't saving">
    If your edits don't appear to be saving, try the following steps in order:

    1. **Check your internet connection.** The editor requires an active connection to sync your work to the server.
    2. **Check the save status indicator** in the top bar. It will show whether your last save succeeded or encountered an error.
    3. **Save manually** by pressing **Ctrl+S** (Windows) or **Cmd+S** (Mac).
    4. If the problem persists, **copy all of your text** from the editor, then reload the page and paste it back in. This clears any temporary session issues.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Images won't upload">
    Before uploading, verify the following:

    - Your image is in a supported format: **JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP**.
    - The file size is **under 5 MB**.
    - Your browser is set to **allow file uploads from story.app**. Check your browser's site permissions if you're unsure.

    If your file meets all these requirements and the upload still fails, try a different browser or a different network connection.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The editor is slow or unresponsive">
    A sluggish editor is usually caused by browser-side issues. Try these steps:

    1. **Clear your browser cache.** Stale cached data can conflict with the editor's scripts.
    2. **Disable browser extensions**, especially ad blockers or script managers, which can interfere with the editor.
    3. **Switch to a Chromium-based browser** (such as Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge) for the best performance and compatibility with the Story editor.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Publishing Issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My story says 'Published' but I can't find it">
    This is normal. After you publish, it can take **a few minutes** for your story to appear in search results and recommendations as our index updates. In the meantime, use the **direct link** from your story's settings page to confirm that it is live and publicly accessible.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't publish my story">
    Free accounts are limited to **5 published stories** at a time. If you've reached that limit, you have two options:

    - **Upgrade to a paid plan** from **Settings → Billing** to unlock unlimited published stories.
    - **Unpublish an existing story** to free up a slot, then publish your new one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account Issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't log in">
    If you're unable to log in to your account, try the following:

    1. Click **Forgot Password** on the login page and request a password reset link.
    2. **Check your spam or junk folder** — the reset email sometimes gets filtered.
    3. Make sure you're entering the **correct email address** associated with your Story account. If you signed up with a social login (Google, Apple), use that option instead of the email form.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm not receiving email notifications">
    If Story emails aren't reaching your inbox:

    1. Go to **Settings → Notifications** and confirm that email notifications are turned on for the events you care about.
    2. **Check your spam folder** for messages from Story.
    3. **Add noreply@story.app to your contacts** to prevent future messages from being filtered.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
